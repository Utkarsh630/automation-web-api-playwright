Scenario A1: Register with valid details
  Given I am on the home page
  When I signup with a unique name and email
  And I fill in the account information form
  And I submit the account creation
  Then I should see "Account Created!"
  And I should be logged in as that user

Scenario A2: Login with valid credentials
  Given I have a registered account
  When I login with the correct email and password
  Then I should see "Logged in as <name>"

Scenario A3: Login with invalid credentials
  Given I am on the login page
  When I login with an incorrect email or password
  Then I should see the error "Your email or password is incorrect!"

Scenario A4: Logout
  Given I am logged in
  When I click Logout
  Then I should be on the login page
